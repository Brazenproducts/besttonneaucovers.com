Best Chevy Colorado Tonneau Covers 2026

Top picks for 5ft and 6ft beds — 2015-2024 Colorado models

The Chevy Colorado is GM's midsize workhorse, and the tonneau cover market for it is solid — not as deep as the full-size trucks, but the major brands all make Colorado-specific fitments. The second-generation Colorado (2015-2022) and the third-gen (2023-2024) share similar bed dimensions but have different rail profiles, so always confirm your model year when ordering.

The Colorado comes in two bed lengths: the 5ft short bed (crew cab) and the 6ft long bed (extended cab). The 5ft bed is far more common, but both are well-supported. One thing to watch: the Colorado's bed rails are narrower than full-size trucks, so covers designed for the Silverado will not fit. Always buy Colorado-specific.

★★★★★ 4.5/5
~$290–$340 for 5ft Colorado

The leather-grain vinyl fabric is durable and cleans easily. TruXedo's hook-and-loop seal runs the full length of both rails, which keeps water out better than most soft covers in this price range. Colorado owners report minimal flapping at highway speeds once the tension is properly adjusted.

The Bottom Line

5ft bed: BAKFlip MX4 for hard fold, TruXedo TruXport for soft roll-up, Tyger Auto T1 for budget. 6ft bed: Extang Solid Fold 2.0 for hard fold, Gator ETX for value. The Colorado's narrower bed rails mean you must buy Colorado-specific covers — Silverado covers will not fit. Always confirm your model year: 2015-2022 and 2023-2024 have different rail profiles.

Colorado Fitment Tips

  • 2015–2022 (2nd Gen): Most covers available, well-established fitment
  • 2023–2024 (3rd Gen): New rail profile — verify compatibility
  • 5ft Bed: Crew cab config, most popular, widest cover selection
  • 6ft Bed: Extended cab, fewer options but still well-supported
  • Note: Colorado rails are narrower than Silverado — do not cross-fit
