2022+ Tundra vs Pre-2022: Fitment Notes

Important Fitment Information

The 2022+ Toyota Tundra is a complete redesign with different bed dimensions than the 2007–2021 generation. When ordering any tonneau cover for your Tundra, always verify:

  • Model year: 2022+ vs 2007–2021 — different bed profiles
  • Bed length: 5.5ft, 6.5ft, or 8.1ft
  • Bed features: Check for deck rail system compatibility
  • Tailgate: 2022+ Tundra has a different tailgate design

The Tundra Tonneau Cover Bottom Line

For most Tundra owners, the BAKFlip MX4 (hard fold) or TruXedo TruXport (soft roll-up) are the two best starting points. The 5.5ft bed is best served by the BAKFlip MX4 if budget allows, or the Gator ETX for value. The 6.5ft bed is the sweet spot for the TruXedo TruXport. If you want the ultimate convenience, the RetraxPRO MX retractable is worth the premium. For general truck accessory recommendations, see our Best Truck Accessories guide.
