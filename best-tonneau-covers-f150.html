Best Tonneau Covers for Ford F-150

Top picks for 5.5ft, 6.5ft, and 8ft beds — ranked by bed length

The Ford F-150 is the best-selling truck in America, which means the tonneau cover market is flooded with options specifically designed for its bed dimensions. The good news: fitment for the F-150 is generally excellent across all major brands. The bad news: you still need to pick the right cover type and the right brand for your specific bed length and how you use your truck.

Key F-150 notes: the 5.5ft (Short Bed) is the most popular configuration. The 6.5ft (Styleside) is next. The 8ft (Heavy Duty payload package) is less common but still well-supported. Make sure you confirm your bed length before ordering — F-150s built between 2015–2020 have slightly different rail geometries than 2021+ models with the fully-boxed bed design.

F-150 Quick Tips

  • 2015–2020: Traditional bed rail design, most covers fit
  • 2021+: Fully-boxed bed — verify fitment before ordering
  • 5.5ft: Most popular, best cover availability
  • 6.5ft: Most flexibility — all cover types work
  • 8ft: Fewer options, budget covers more limited
