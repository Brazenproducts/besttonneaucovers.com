Best Soft Roll-Up Tonneau Covers

Ranked for value, weather protection, ease of use, and long-term ownership

Soft roll-up covers dominate the tonneau market because they hit the sweet spot for most truck owners: affordable, easy to live with, and they get the job done without drama. They roll up in seconds for full bed access, install without drilling on most trucks, and cost a fraction of what hard covers do. The trade-off is lower security and less premium feel, but for a daily-driver truck, that is usually fine.

1. TruXedo TruXport

Editor's Choice

TruXedo TruXport Soft Roll-Up

★★★★★ 4.6/5
~$280–$380

The TruXedo TruXport is the benchmark soft roll-up for a reason. It has a longer brand history than most competitors, installs cleanly without drilling, and the tension control system keeps it snug without constant adjustment. The price sits above budget options but below premium territory, and the ownership experience reflects that.

✓ Pros

  • Best balance of price and refinement in the category
  • Wide truck fitment availability
  • Tension adjuster reduces sagging over time
  • Straightforward no-drill install

✗ Cons

  • Velcro side seals need periodic re-engagement
  • Vinyl is less premium than hard covers
  • Security is limited vs folding or retractable covers

2. Gator ETX Soft Roll-Up

Best Value

Gator ETX Soft Roll-Up

★★★★★ 4.5/5
~$230–$320

The Gator ETX is the smarter buy if you want the soft-cover experience without paying TruXedo prices. It uses a similar design — roll-up operation, clamp-on rails, hook-and-loop seals — and performs close enough that most owners cannot tell the difference. Great for a truck that gets used hard and washed often.

✓ Pros

  • Strong price-to-performance ratio
  • Reliable weather sealing for daily use
  • Fast roll-up and full bed access
  • Low maintenance once installed

✗ Cons

  • Slightly less refined hardware than TruXedo
  • Can develop minor sag in extreme heat over time
