Best Ford Ranger Tonneau Covers 2026

Top picks for the 5ft bed — 2019-2024 Ranger models

The Ford Ranger returned to the US market in 2019 after an eight-year hiatus, and the tonneau cover market caught up fast. Every major brand now makes Ranger-specific covers for the 5ft SuperCrew bed. The Ranger only comes in one bed length in the US — 5ft (approximately 61 inches) — which simplifies your decision. You are picking cover type and brand, not bed size.

Important fitment note: the 2019-2023 Ranger and the redesigned 2024 Ranger have different bed rail profiles. The 2024 model is wider and uses a new mounting system. Most covers marketed as "2019-2024" actually only fit 2019-2023. If you have a 2024 Ranger, double-check that the listing explicitly includes the new body style before ordering.

TruXedo TruXport — Ford Ranger 5ft Bed

★★★★★ 4.6/5
~$280–$330 for Ranger 5ft

The TruXedo TruXport is the most popular soft roll-up for the Ranger, and for good reason. The 5ft bed is short enough that the rolled-up cover sits tight against the cab without eating into cargo space. The leather-grain vinyl looks sharp and the full-length hook-and-loop seal keeps water out of the bed.

The TruXport's tension control system is important on the Ranger because the 5ft bed can develop a slight bow in the vinyl if the tension is not set correctly. Once adjusted, the cover sits flat and does not flap at highway speeds. Installation takes about 15 minutes — the fastest install of any cover on this list.

Best Premium

RetraxPRO XR — Ford Ranger 5ft Bed

★★★★★ 4.6/5
~$1,700–$1,900 for Ranger 5ft

If you want the cleanest possible look on your Ranger and security is a priority, the RetraxPRO XR is the retractable to get. It slides open and closed on a ball-bearing track, locks at any position, and retracts into a canister at the front of the bed. The Bottom Line

Best overall: BAKFlip MX4 for hard fold, TruXedo TruXport for soft roll-up. Best value: Extang Trifecta 2.0 bridges the gap between soft and hard. Budget: Rough Country gets the basics right under $260. Premium: RetraxPRO XR if security and aesthetics justify the price. Remember: the 2024 Ranger has a different bed profile than 2019-2023 — always confirm fitment for your specific model year.

Ranger Fitment Tips

  • 2019–2023: Same bed rail profile, most covers fit all years
  • 2024 (Redesign): New wider bed — verify cover compatibility
  • Bed Length: 5ft only (US market) — approximately 61 inches
  • Bed Width: ~44.8 inches between wheel wells
  • Note: F-150 covers will NOT fit the Ranger
