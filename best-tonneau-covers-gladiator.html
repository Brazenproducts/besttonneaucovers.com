Best Jeep Gladiator Tonneau Covers 2026

Top picks for the 5ft bed — 2020-2025 Gladiator models

The Jeep Gladiator is not like other midsize trucks. Its bed dimensions are unique — the 5ft steel bed measures approximately 60 inches long, but the bed width between the wheel wells is narrower than competitors like the Tacoma or Colorado at roughly 44.8 inches. The bed rails also sit higher relative to the bed floor, and the Gladiator's trail rail system (the integrated tie-down track) can interfere with some cover mounting systems. All of this means you need a Gladiator-specific tonneau cover. Do not try to cross-fit from another truck.

The good news: because the Gladiator has been on the market since 2020 with virtually no changes to the bed design through 2025, fitment is consistent across all model years. A cover that fits a 2020 Gladiator will fit a 2025 Gladiator. The bed dimensions have not changed, and the rail profile is identical. This makes shopping easier — you just need to pick the right cover type for how you use the truck.

~$290–$350 for Gladiator

The TruXedo TruXport is the best soft roll-up for the Gladiator. The 5ft bed is compact enough that the rolled-up cover sits tight against the cab without eating into usable cargo space. The leather-grain vinyl is durable and the full-length hook-and-loop seal keeps water and trail dust out of the bed — important for a truck that actually goes off-road.

Installation on the Gladiator takes about 20 minutes. The TruXport's mounting system works with the Gladiator's bed rails without conflicting with the trail rail system. Once dialed in, it sits flat and quiet at highway speeds.
