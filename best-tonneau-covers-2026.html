How to Think About This Category

Hard fold covers usually win on security and a more premium feel. Soft roll-up covers win on price, simplicity, and ease of use. Retractable covers are the slickest but also the easiest place to overspend. The right pick depends on whether you care most about security, weather resistance, appearance, or price.
