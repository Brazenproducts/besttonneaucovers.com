Best Tonneau Covers for RAM 1500

Top picks for 5'7" and 6'4" beds — including RamBox-compatible options

The RAM 1500 has two bed lengths: the 5'7" Short Bed and the 6'4" Standard Bed. Both are well-supported by every major tonneau brand. The wrinkle with the RAM 1500 is RamBox — the integrated storage compartments built into the bed sides on crew cab and Mega Cab models. If your RAM has RamBox, you need covers specifically designed or verified to work with that system.

Best for 5'7" (Short Bed) RAM 1500

Editor's Choice

★★★★★ 4.6/5
~$950–$1,100 for 5'7" RAM 1500

The MX4 is the most-recommended hard fold for the 5'7" RAM 1500. It fits cleanly, the clamps are well-suited to the RAM's bed rail design, and the Matte Black finish looks good on the truck. The Bottom Line

5'7" bed: BAKFlip MX4 for hard fold security, Gator ETX for value. 6'4" bed: TruXedo TruXport for the best all-around soft cover experience, Tyger Auto T1 for budget. RamBox models: Stick with soft roll-up covers explicitly listed as RamBox-compatible — hard fold and retractable covers may interfere with the storage compartment geometry.

RAM 1500 Quick Tips

  • 5'7" Short Bed: Most popular config, best cover availability
  • 6'4" Standard Bed: All cover types work well
  • RamBox: Use soft roll-up covers listed as RamBox-compatible
  • 2019+ RAM 1500: Fifth-generation design — verify fitment for your model year
