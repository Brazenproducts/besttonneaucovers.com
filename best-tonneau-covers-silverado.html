Best Tonneau Covers for Silverado & GMC Sierra

Short bed and standard bed — top picks for GM trucks ranked

The Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra share the same bed dimensions, which means most tonneau covers fit both trucks interchangeably. The 2019+ generation (Silverado 1500) and the heavy-duty models (2500/3500) use different bed dimensions, so make sure you are looking at the right truck. The 1500 short bed is approximately 5'10", and the standard bed is approximately 6'6" — slightly different from Ford and RAM equivalents.
