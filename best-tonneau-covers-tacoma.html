Best Tonneau Covers for Toyota Tacoma

3rd Gen (2016–2023) and 4th Gen (2024+) — 5ft and 6ft beds

The Toyota Tacoma is one of the most popular mid-size trucks, and the tonneau cover market has caught up. Fitment for the Tacoma has historically been messier than full-size trucks due to the narrower bed and different rail geometries, but every major brand now lists Tacoma-specific SKUs. The critical distinction is the 3rd generation (2016–2023) vs the all-new 4th generation (2024+) — they share the same bed lengths but have different rail dimensions due to the redesigned bed structure on the 2024+ model.

The Bottom Line

3rd Gen (2016–2023) 5ft bed: BAKFlip MX4 for hard fold, Gator ETX for value. 3rd Gen 6ft bed: TruXedo TruXport for best soft cover, Tyger Auto T1 for budget. 4th Gen (2024+): Verify 2024+ specific fitment listings before ordering — the redesigned bed uses different rail dimensions. Do not assume a 3rd gen part will fit a 4th gen truck.

Tacoma Quick Tips

  • 3rd Gen (2016–2023): Proven fitment for all major brands
  • 4th Gen (2024+): New bed design — must verify 2024+ specific listings
  • 5ft Bed: Most popular Tacoma config
  • 6ft Bed: Less common but all cover types work
  • Mid-size consideration: Covers look slightly bulkier relative to bed size vs full-size trucks
